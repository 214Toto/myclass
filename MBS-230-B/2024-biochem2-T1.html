2. A new drug is developed which selectively cleaves covalent bonds between two sulfur atoms of non-adjacent amino acids in a polypeptide chain.

Model Answers

i. Tertiary structure (and potentially Quaternary structure).

ii. The drug cleaves disulfide bonds (covalent bonds between two sulfur atoms). These bonds are critical for stabilizing the three-dimensional folding of a single polypeptide chain (tertiary) or the interaction between multiple subunits (quaternary).

iii.

  • Primary: The linear sequence of amino acids in a polypeptide chain.
  • Secondary: Local folding patterns such as alpha-helices and beta-pleated sheets.
  • Quaternary: The spatial arrangement and association of multiple polypeptide subunits.

iv.

  • Primary: Peptide bonds (covalent).
  • Secondary: Hydrogen bonds between backbone atoms.
  • Tertiary/Quaternary: Hydrophobic interactions, hydrogen bonds, ionic bonds (salt bridges), and disulfide bridges.
