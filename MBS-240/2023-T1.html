Short Essay Questions

1. Describe the physiological responses that occur following an acute loss of blood volume.

Model Answers

a) Rapid sympathetic activation increases heart rate and contractility; peripheral vasoconstriction raises systemic vascular resistance to maintain perfusion.

b) Release of catecholamines, ADH secretion and activation of RAAS promote vasoconstriction and fluid retention to support blood pressure.

c) Kidneys increase erythropoietin to stimulate erythropoiesis; renal sodium/water retention and mobilization of interstitial fluid restore plasma volume over days to weeks.

2. Explain the kidney's role in maintaining electrolyte and fluid balance.

Model Answers

a) The proximal tubule reabsorbs the bulk of filtered sodium and water via coupled transporters and passive water movement; most solute and isoosmotic fluid is reclaimed here.

b) Aldosterone acts on distal tubule and collecting duct principal cells to increase expression of ENaC and Na+/K+ ATPase, enhancing sodium reabsorption and potassium secretion.

c) ADH increases water permeability of the collecting duct by inserting aquaporin-2 channels, concentrating urine when the body is dehydrated and reducing free-water excretion.
